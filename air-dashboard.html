How is Air Pollution a Health Risk?

Air pollution contains harmful particles such as sulfur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide. These pollutants can enter the body through breathing and cause serious health issues.

Short-term Effects

  • Asthma attacks
  • Irritation of eyes, nose and throat
  • Chest pain

Long-term Effects

  • Heart disease
  • Lung disease
  • Lung cancer

Who is Most Vulnerable?

Children

Children are more vulnerable because their lungs are still developing and they breathe faster.

Pregnant Women

Air pollution has been linked to premature birth and health risks for babies.

Elderly People

Older people have weaker immune and respiratory systems.

People with Pre-existing Conditions

Conditions like asthma, diabetes and heart disease can worsen due to pollution.

How Can We Reduce the Impact?

Individual Actions

  • Walk or use public transport
  • Avoid burning fires

Government Actions

  • Introduce ULEZ zones
  • Improve public transport

Environmental Actions

  • Plant more trees
