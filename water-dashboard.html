Water Information

What is Water Pollution?

Water pollution happens when harmful substances such as toxic chemicals and microorganisms enter water sources. These pollutants are often invisible and make water unsafe for drinking and use.

Main Sources of Water Pollution

  • Agricultural runoff
  • Urban stormwater runoff
  • Industrial discharge

Effects of Water Pollution

  • Diseases: Cholera, Hepatitis A, Dysentery, Diarrhoea
  • Lack of potable water
  • Contamination of the food chain
  • Infant mortality — diarrhoeal diseases linked to poor hygiene cause around 1,000 child deaths per day worldwide

Ways to Reduce Water Pollution

  • Dispose of household waste (medicines, chemicals, wastewater) correctly
  • Reduce use of harmful chemicals in daily life
  • Reduce single-use plastics
  • Avoid excessive use of fertilisers and pesticides

Water Data Features

  • Water Levels: Nearby rivers, streams, and lakes
  • River Conditions: Currents, storm overflows, sewage discharge points
  • Flood Warnings: Local flood risk levels and alerts
  • Water Quality: Monitoring of nearby swimming and drinking water safety
  • Water Safety Tips: Tide conditions, cold weather risks, and current hazards
